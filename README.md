# Bids AI — from bid solicitation to priced proposal

Medical-supply distributors answer a steady stream of public-sector and health-system solicitations:
VA medical centers, the Defense Logistics Agency, prisons and state corrections, public-health
departments. Each one arrives as a PDF, Word or Excel package that mixes legal terms with dozens to
hundreds of product lines. Today a bids team reads every page, routes clauses to legal and
compliance, matches each line to the catalog by hand, checks past bids and purchase history to
price it, and retypes it all into a quote. That takes days, and a missed clause or a mismatched
item can lose the bid.

**Bids AI turns that package into a reviewed, priced proposal in one sitting**, while people stay
in charge of every decision.

## What the demo shows

A five-step reviewer workbench: **Upload → Requirements → Line items → Match → Generate**.

1. **Upload** a solicitation (or pick one of 8 samples). The app parses it, page by page.
2. **Requirements:** terms and conditions are extracted, routed to the owning team (Business,
   Corporate Legal, Compliance, …) with a risk level, each linked to its source page.
3. **Line items:** every requested product, quantity and unit is extracted.
4. **Match:** each line is matched to the item catalog with keyword and semantic search, ranked
   with the customer's bid and purchase history, and priced. Low-confidence matches are held for
   review instead of being auto-selected.
5. **Generate** the customer-ready proposal as PDF and Excel.

## The data

All synthetic and generated by the demo: an item catalog of 119 medical, dental and infection-control
products from 24 manufacturers, 1,200 historical bids and 900 purchase-history records across 20
customer accounts and 7 divisions, and 8 sample solicitations (PDF, XLSX, DOCX). A new demo opens
with four bids built from those samples, each at a different stage of review.

## Why Databricks

- **AI Functions** (`ai_parse_document`, `ai_extract`, `ai_classify`) and **Claude Sonnet 5.5** on
  Model Serving read the documents.
- **Lakebase** (managed Postgres) holds the catalog, the bid history and every bid in progress, and
  **Lakebase Search** (BM25 + vector indexes) does the matching inside the same database.
- **Unity Catalog** governs the bid documents; one **Asset Bundle** deploys the demo into an
  existing app, Lakebase project and schema.
- The app's **Databricks assets** menu opens each of these in the workspace, so the audience can see
  what powers every screen.

**Outcome:** bid responses go from days of reading and lookups to same-day turnaround, and every
clause and match stays traceable to its source page for review.

## Try it

Needs an existing app, Lakebase project and UC schema (default names `bids-ai-demo` and `bids_ai`);
[setup.md](docs/setup.md#prerequisites) lists their configuration and the commands to create them.

```bash
python3 src/bootstrap/setup_demo.py --target dev      # deploy the demo (safe to re-run)
python3 src/bootstrap/teardown_demo.py --target dev   # remove what setup created
```

`--target dev` deploys to the builder's workspace and `--target hls` to the HLS demo workspace, where
the demo already runs. For another workspace, add a target to `databricks.yml`.
